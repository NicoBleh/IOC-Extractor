SUMMARY
-------
Between April 28 and May 9, 2026, our team observed a targeted
attack campaign against several mid-sized companies in the DACH
region. Initial access was gained via a spear-phishing wave,
followed by exploitation of a known vulnerability (CVE-2024-3400)
in externally exposed gateways. We attribute the activity with
medium confidence to the group "DUSK PELICAN".

INITIAL ACCESS
--------------
The attackers sent spoofed job-application emails from the address
hr-team@mail-secure-login[.]example. A second sender address used
in the later phase was invoice@example-vendor.example. The emails
contained a manipulated PDF document (SHA256:
e3b0c44298fc1c149afbf4c8996fb92427ae41e4649b934ca495991b7852b855),
which downloaded a loader upon opening.

The download was fetched from the defanged URL
hxxps://update-server[.]example/api/v2/beacon and as a fallback
from hxxp://cdn-sync[.]example/files/loader.bin. The associated
command-and-control infrastructure was reachable at 198.51.100.23
at the time of observation; a second node responded at
203.0.113[.]47 on port 8443.

PERSISTENCE AND LATERAL MOVEMENT
---------------------------------
After establishing persistence, the malware created the registry key
HKCU\Software\Microsoft\Windows\CurrentVersion\Run\SyncHelper and
wrote its payload to
C:\Users\Public\Documents\synchelper.exe. The corresponding
MD5 hash of this file is 44d88612fea8a8f36de82e1278abb02f,
and the SHA1 hash is da39a3ee5e6b4b0d3255bfef95601890afd80709.

For lateral movement within the network, the attackers used stolen
credentials and communicated with an additional server at 192.0.2.150.
In one instance, data exfiltration to the domain cdn-sync[.]example
was observed, resolving to the address 198.51.100.99.

ADDITIONAL INDICATORS
---------------------
An additional malicious file was identified under the SHA256 hash
9f86d081884c7d659a2feaa0c55ad015a3bf4f1b2b0b822cd15d6c15b0f00a08.
The group also exploited the vulnerability CVE-2023-23397,
previously documented in earlier campaigns, for privilege escalation.
